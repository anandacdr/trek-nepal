Why trek with us

Local guides. Small groups. No surprises.

01

Sherpa-led, always

Every trip is run by a licensed guide from the region you're walking through — not a tour leader flown in for the season.

02

Acclimatisation built in

Rest days aren't padding. They're the reason people reach Base Camp instead of turning round at Dingboche with a headache.

03

One price, everything in

Permits, TIMS card, lodging, porters and internal flights. The number you see is the number you pay.

The people who make it possible

Nothing up here arrives by road.

Every lodge bed, gas cylinder and crate of eggs above Lukla was carried up on somebody's back. Our porters are weight-limited, insured, equipped and lodged indoors — and we will show you the policy before you book.
